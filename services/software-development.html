Software that shares a backbone with your ERP

The usual pattern is familiar: an ERP for the back office, a separate app for the field team, and a nightly sync job that someone has to babysit. Every mismatch between the two becomes an argument about which system is right.

Building the application on the same Frappe backbone removes that argument. One database, one permission model, one audit trail — and a mobile app that writes directly into the records your accounts team is already looking at.

We build portals for customers and suppliers, internal tools that replace long-running spreadsheets, and mobile apps for people who work away from a desk. Where a separate stack genuinely suits the job better, we say so and integrate properly instead.

FAQ

Questions we are asked before signing

Do we need ERPNext to work with you on an application?

No. We build on Frappe because it gives us a tested permission model, workflow engine and REST layer out of the box, and that is useful whether or not you run ERPNext. If you do run it, the integration is simply free.

Are the mobile apps native or web-based?

We scope this per project. Most field applications work well as installable progressive web apps with offline capture; genuine native builds are worth it when the app needs deep hardware access such as barcode or camera pipelines.

Who hosts the application?

Either you or us. Many clients run on their own cloud account so that infrastructure stays in their name. We set up the environments, backups and monitoring either way.

What happens when we need changes later?

Most clients move to a monthly retainer after launch. You keep a prioritised backlog; we work through it in agreed cycles. There is no obligation to keep us on to be able to change your own software.
