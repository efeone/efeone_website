How the services fit together

Most engagements start in one of two places. Either a business already knows it wants ERPNext and needs it implemented, or it knows something is wrong with how the work flows and wants that examined first. Those are the implementation and re-engineering services respectively, and the second frequently leads into the first.

Customisation and software development come next, because almost every business has a handful of things it does differently from everyone else. We hold these back deliberately until the standard system is running, since the cheapest custom app is the one that turns out not to be needed.

Support and AMC is where the relationship settles. An ERP that nobody touches after go-live slowly stops matching the business, and the annual contract exists to keep the two in step rather than to wait for faults.
