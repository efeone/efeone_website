Privacy policy

How efeone collects, uses and protects personal information. Last updated 1 Oct 2026.

What we collect

We collect personal information only when you give it to us — through the enquiry form on this site, by email, or in the course of an engagement. That is typically your name, company, email address, phone number and whatever you choose to tell us about your business.

This site also records basic analytics: pages visited, approximate location, device and browser, and how you arrived. This is used to understand which pages are useful, not to identify you individually.

How we use it

Enquiry details are used to respond to you and, if we work together, to deliver and support that work. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except where a service we use to run our business requires it — email hosting and analytics, for example — or where the law requires it.

Client data

During an engagement we may be given access to systems holding your business and personal data. We access it only as needed for the work, keep it on systems with controlled access, and return or delete it on request at the end of the engagement. Specific obligations are set out in the agreement between us.

Cookies

This site uses cookies for analytics. You can block or delete them in your browser; the site works without them.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept while there is an active conversation and for a reasonable period afterwards. Records relating to a contract are kept for as long as our statutory and tax obligations require.

Your rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to info@efeone.com and we will respond within a reasonable period.
