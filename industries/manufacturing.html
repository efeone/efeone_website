The problem

“Nobody knows what a job actually cost.”

Why manufacturers choose ERPNext

Manufacturing is where generic ERP configurations usually fall over. Every plant has its own way of handling rework, scrap, byproducts and subcontracting, and the standard configuration rarely matches it on day one.

Because ERPNext is open source, the parts that do not fit can be built rather than worked around with a spreadsheet beside the system. We have written custom doctypes for batch-driven costing, barcode-based issue and receipt, and production reports that managers actually read.

And with no per-user licence fee, putting a terminal on the shop floor costs nothing extra — which is usually what makes real-time material booking possible in the first place.

FAQ

Questions we are asked before signing

Does ERPNext handle multi-level BOMs and subcontracting?

Yes. Nested BOMs, scrap items, operation-level routing and full subcontracting with supplier-held stock are all standard. We configure them against your actual routing during discovery.

Can shop-floor staff use it without training on accounting?

Yes. Operators get a job-card screen, usually with barcode scanning, and see nothing else. Role permissions keep the ledger out of their way.

Will it connect to our machines or weighbridge?

Where the equipment exposes a feed or an API, yes — we have built integrations for weighing systems and barcode hardware. We scope this explicitly, because it is the part most often assumed and never specified.

How long before production data is reliable?

Usually one to two months after go-live. The system is correct from day one; the discipline of booking material and time as it happens takes a few weeks to settle, and we support that period deliberately.
