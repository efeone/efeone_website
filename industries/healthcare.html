Why ERPNext suits mid-sized providers

Large hospital information systems are priced and scoped for large hospitals. A clinic chain or a mid-sized hospital usually ends up either paying for capability it will never use, or running a cheap patient system alongside a separate accounting package and reconciling between them.

ERPNext Healthcare covers the clinical workflow on the same platform as the accounts, stock and purchasing, which removes that reconciliation entirely. Consumables issued during a procedure reduce stock and appear on the bill because they are the same transaction.

Patient data demands care. We configure role-based permission so that clinical records are visible only to those who need them, keep the audit trail on, and set up backups that are verified by restore rather than assumed.

FAQ

Questions we are asked before signing

Is ERPNext suitable for a multi-branch clinic chain?

Yes. Branches are modelled as companies or cost centres depending on how you report, with shared patient records and separate stock and accounts per location. The right structure depends on your statutory reporting, so we settle it during discovery.

How is patient data protected?

Through role-based permissions, record-level restrictions and the platform audit trail, on infrastructure you control. We configure least-privilege access rather than relying on the convention that nobody looks at what they should not.

Does it track drug batches and expiry?

Yes, with batch and expiry handling through dispensing, so near-expiry stock can be reported on and acted upon instead of found during stock-take.

Can it integrate with lab machines or insurance portals?

Where the equipment or portal exposes an interface, yes. These integrations are scoped individually because the protocols vary considerably, and an unexamined assumption here is expensive.
