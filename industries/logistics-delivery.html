Why the field and the office belong on one system

Every delivery business eventually builds a second system by accident: a WhatsApp group, a shared sheet, a daily call. It exists because the real system does not reach the people doing the work, and it is where the operation’s actual state lives.

Putting the run on the driver’s phone removes the need for it. The office sees a delivery the moment it happens, a short delivery is recorded with a reason at the door, and the customer call is answered from the screen rather than by ringing the van.

Because it writes into the same ERP that raised the invoice, settlement is a comparison rather than a reconstruction: what was collected, what was invoiced and what was banked are three views of one record.

FAQ

Questions we are asked before signing

Does the driver app work without a network?

Yes. The run is downloaded at the start of the day and deliveries are captured locally, syncing when a connection returns. On most rural routes this is a requirement rather than a nicety.

Can customers track their delivery?

Yes, through a link showing status and the expected window. Full live vehicle tracking needs a telematics device, which we integrate with where you already have one.

How are returns and short deliveries handled?

The driver records them at the stop with a reason code, which creates the credit or return document in the ERP immediately. The dispute conversation then starts from a timestamped record rather than two recollections.

Can we use it with vans selling directly on the route?

Yes. Van sales are supported by modelling each vehicle as a warehouse, so loading, sales, returns and settlement all reconcile against the stock that went out that morning.
