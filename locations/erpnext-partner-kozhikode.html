Talk to us

Working with a local ERP team

We are based at Carino INC, Pooladikkunnu, Eranhikkal, Kozhikode, and have built the practice around businesses in and around Kozhikode — manufacturers in the industrial estates, distributors serving Malabar, hospitals and clinics, and contractors working across the district.

Proximity changes how an implementation runs. Discovery that would take three calls takes one morning on site. A go-live is covered in person rather than over a screen share. When something breaks during month-end close, someone can be there.

We also work with clients across Kerala and in the Gulf, where the same work runs remotely with planned on-site visits at the points that matter — discovery, training and cut-over.
