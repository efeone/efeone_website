ERP for Kerala businesses

Kerala’s mid-sized businesses sit in an awkward gap. They have outgrown accounting software and spreadsheets, but the ERP products pitched at them are priced per user and scoped for far larger organisations, which makes the economics hard to justify.

Open-source ERP changes that arithmetic. ERPNext covers accounting, stock, manufacturing, sales, purchase, projects and HR with no per-user licence fee, so the cost sits in implementation and support rather than in a bill that grows every time you hire.

For Indian businesses the compliance side matters as much as the features. GST invoicing, e-invoicing and e-way bills are handled through the India Compliance app, which we configure as part of every implementation rather than treating as an add-on.

FAQ

Questions we are asked before signing

Which parts of Kerala do you cover?

All of it. We are based in Kozhikode and work on site across the state, planning visits around discovery, training and cut-over.

Does ERPNext handle GST properly?

Yes. GST invoicing, e-invoicing, e-way bills and the returns are supported through the India Compliance app, which is actively maintained and widely used by Indian businesses.

Is ERPNext suitable for a business with 20 to 200 staff?

That is the band where it fits best. Below that, simpler accounting software is often enough; well above it, the question becomes one of internal IT capacity rather than of the platform.

Can you train staff in Malayalam?

Yes. Training and support are delivered in Malayalam or English depending on who is in the room, which matters most for shop-floor and warehouse roles.

Tell us what is slowing your business down.

A 30-minute call, no slide deck. You describe the process; we tell you honestly whether ERPNext is the right answer.
