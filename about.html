About us

An ERP team in Kozhikode, working on open source

efeone builds and supports ERP systems on ERPNext and the Frappe framework for businesses in Kerala, across India and in the GCC.

What we do and why

We are a technology consultancy specialising in ERPNext, an open-source ERP platform. Our work runs from mapping how a business actually operates, through implementing and customising the system, to supporting it for years afterwards.

We chose open source deliberately. A business that runs on proprietary ERP rents its own operational data, and the cost of leaving rises every year. On ERPNext there are no per-user licence fees, the source is readable, and anything we build for a client belongs to that client. If they ever want to work with someone else, nothing stops them.

We work in the open in other ways too. Our team takes part in the Indian free and open-source community, including India FOSS, and contributes back to the platform we build on.

How we work

Four commitments we hold to

Process before software

We map how the work runs before configuring anything, because automating an unexamined process preserves its faults.

Standard before custom

Standard ERPNext gets upgraded and tested by a community. Custom code becomes someone&rsquo;s responsibility forever, so we build it only where it earns its place.

Your code is yours

Custom work is delivered to your repository and your site. The relationship should continue because it works, not because leaving is hard.

Say the difficult thing early

If a scope will not hold, or ERPNext is the wrong answer, we would rather say so in week two than in month five.
